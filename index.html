🔐 Four Main Cybersecurity Pillars

🔒 Confidentiality

Confidentiality means keeping information private and allowing only authorized people to access it.

  • Protect passwords
  • Protect personal information
  • Use secure access controls
  • Don't share sensitive information

🛡️ Integrity

Integrity means keeping information accurate and protecting it from unauthorized changes.

  • Protect important files
  • Prevent unauthorized modification
  • Verify information
  • Keep backups

⚡ Availability

Availability means making sure systems and information are accessible when authorized users need them.

  • Keep systems updated
  • Create backups
  • Maintain reliable systems
  • Protect against service disruption

👤 Authentication & Accountability

Authentication verifies who a user is, while accountability helps associate actions with the responsible user.

  • Use strong passwords
  • Enable MFA/2FA
  • Monitor login activity
  • Use activity logs

⚠️ Common Cyber Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake messages designed to trick people into giving information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software designed to harm or compromise systems.

🔐 Ransomware

Malware that can prevent access to files or systems.

👤 Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

🌐 DDoS

An attempt to make an online service unavailable by overwhelming it with traffic.

🪪 Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without authorization.

🛡️ Cybersecurity Safety Tips

  • 🔑 Use strong and unique passwords.
  • 🔐 Enable two-factor authentication.
  • 🚫 Never share OTPs or passwords.
  • 🎣 Don't click suspicious links.
  • 🔄 Keep software updated.
  • 💾 Back up important files.
  • 📎 Be careful with unknown attachments.
  • 📱 Lock your devices.
  • 🌐 Check website URLs before entering sensitive information.
